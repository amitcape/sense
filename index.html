Transition Partner in Innovation (TP-I)

In this era of accelerating innovation, “we must run as fast as we can, just to stay in place. And if you wish to go anywhere you must run twice as fast as that.” (Red Queen’s Race) In a tight economy, we work with you to identify how best you can create an innovation portfolio with the least disruption to your core business and with minimal associated costs.

Transition Partner in Digitisation (TP-D)

Digitisation is a key enabler of both profit and prosperity across industries. Whether you're operating a small foundry looking to optimise your supply chain or managing a major mining facility that needs real-time insights into metal quality, digitisation helps you maximise profit while minimising waste. This transformation relies on two critical technologies: sensors and AI, which represent the core expertise of Sens-AI.

Transition Partner in Regulation-Adherence (TP-R)

Revolutionary technologies like AI and ambitious initiatives such as the Green Deal are bringing new regulatory frameworks, including the EU AI Act (being phased in through 2028), the Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM) and the EU's Omnibus simplification reforms. Many of these rules touch sensors and AI, which is our core expertise, and we are deeply passionate about sustainability. We help organisations understand which requirements apply to them and keep their current and future operations compliant as the rules evolve.

Transition Partner in Work-Integrated-Learning (TP-WIL)

We have decades of experience designing both short introductory and in-depth courses on emerging technologies for working professionals. For example, one of our CPD courses on AI has been on offer for multiple years in South Africa. WIL is crucial to both keeping your workforce skilled as well as motivated.

Current Projects

CommSense JCAS Systems

In one of the early patents in the domain of joint communication and sensing (JCAS), we pioneered the use of channel equalisation signals to sense the environment (an innovation which we patented in 2015). We have shown its application in a range of applications starting from detecting wheel-chairs in public places to detecting drones in cluttered backgrounds. If you want to design a system to detect a target or situation of interest, our innovation may mostly be of use.

Sensor Calibration using AI

This project deployed a co-located sensor platform at Cape Point to improve the performance of low-cost air quality sensors. Leveraging expertise in sensor design and blind calibration, advanced AI methods were developed to enhance measurement reliability without relying on reference instruments. The work has been internationally recognised, with invited tutorials delivered at flagship conferences such as IEEE I2MTC 2024, and has led to a patent on innovative calibration approaches (WO2024092293A1). Results have also been published in IEEE I2MTC and IEEE TIM.
